As it's been discussed on Slack, we've decided to update the weekly ticket that automatically posts the most important information about what's happening in the organization.

What changed?
The weekly ticket will now include:
* Slack Higlights from general channels
* Important Mails

Based on one of our values; Transparency, we decided to take it really serious, as we had early issues of members not knowing enough information of what was happening around. Problems are found such as members reading hundred of messages to get the information needed for opening a project, or getting the necessary knowledge for a meeting.

These all problems will be solved with a enchanced weekly ticket, where all the important information is published there.

If you want, you can subscribe to the automated newsletter, so you'll get the ticket information right in your inbox. To subscribe, click here.
