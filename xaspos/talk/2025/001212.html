As stated on our wiki (https://wiki.bbjprojek.org), we started deploying our own gitlab server (available at https://ax.bbjprojek.org). Note that this new server won't replace our main GitLab environment, which is available at https://git.bbjprojek.org.

BBJProjeK GitLab CE (based on the gitlab/gitlab-ce:15.7.3-ce.0 docker branch) is a testing environment and it won't used as our main infrastructure.

If you want to register, please ask for your credentials on #gitlab Slack channel, and we will provide you with your username and one-time password.

You can use the server to store your code, use it to upload wiki and snippets, deploy pages, set up CI/CD pipelines, and more! This without any costs and no limits, unlike our current GitLab session (based on GitLab SaaS, in gitlab.com).

Our self-hosted GitLab env. is already available and will be up for the next months. Then, we will negotiate whether migration from GitLab SaaS is viable. Discuseed on next op. meeting.

Enjoy and happy building!
