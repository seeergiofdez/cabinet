As of 24th November 2025, and governed by 69/2025 RaBBAu Note, Lapiacery and last projects derivated from the team are now officially dissolved, and so used resources has been reassigned for other purposes. These include resources for a faster general infrastructure, including resources for playground.

Beginning with 2024, Lapiacery was deprecated and paused, including all its projects, as well as their main site. This announcement formalizes the reallocation of Lapiacery-associated resources to align with organizational priorities. #lapiacery channel history in Slack has been merged into #legacy.

This is also related to how we reassigned resources with FlaTOR, back in June 2025. A project that didn't align with organizational priorities. Check [Ticket] FlaTOR resources reassigned (RaBBAu-A-2025-27) for more information.

Scope of Reassignment
As same proccedure with FlaTOR; formerly supporting development, compliance and documentation will transition to high-priority projects under BBJProjeK oversight. Systems must adopt current standards.

Documentation
All Lapiacery-related guides (legacy documentation) are archived. Access requires BBJProjeK's approval. Some RaBBAu Notes are archived. The archived notes are repealed by 69/2025 RaBBAu Note.

This was an action we had to make because Lapiacery last actions weren't aligning with BBJProjeK values. We believe in values that allow us to grow as a unified team, build trust with our community, and achieve meaningful results with integrity. Our values are breathing credit we give each other by assuming good intent framework for how we collaborate, decide, and move forward, together.

Questions? Hit up BBJProjeK or check the archives if you’re feeling nostalgic. More information on #legacy on Slack.
