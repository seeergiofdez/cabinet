It's been some weeks since we started discussing about the next possible decentralised development iteration.

The first iteration, The Agency, was up for 700 days. Our current one, BBJProjeK The Authority, has been up for more than 900 days, created on May 2023, and although there are no current plans of shutting it down, we have to look forward to new possibilities and projects.


Adverts format for The Spire. First initial scheme.

Name format
Following the initial planned names (71/2025 RaBBAu Note), the next iteration name would be "The Ruins", but we decided to skip this one and reserve for the death of The Authority.

The planned names are (content from the directive):
* BBJProjeK The Agency (Info)
* BBJProjeK The Authority (Info)
* BBJProjeK The Ruins //skipped//
* BBJProjeK The Spire
* BBJProjeK The Aftermath
* BBJProjeK The Convergence
* BBJProjeK The Pyramid

This list is based off the buildings that appeared on The Apollo Island, in the well-known Center Island.




Some images of The Spire located in Apollo Island.

The new era
BBJProjeK The Spire will be based on current bbjprojek's legal address, and will be focused on security research and software engineering.


New logo format for The Spire. First initial scheme.

It's scheduled to start in the next year, but there's no exact specified date yet. The initial redacted plan describes The Spire to start in the first half of 2026 (26H1).

Following the final roadmap of 2025, The Authority can last about 200 more days, after the halt of 2024.
