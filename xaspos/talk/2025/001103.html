This ticket serves as some information, up to November 6th, 2025, about the iterations in the BBJProjeK organization.

BBJProjeK The Agency
Known just as “The Agency” back in 2018 mainly focused on Android development, it was later renamed to BBJProjeK The Agency, when BBJProjeK was founded. It was effectively dissolved around June, 2021.

Dedicated to the Android developmend. It released several services and products, e.g. ElementOS ROM, PaloKernel, or SafetyNet Builder, as well as different app development

- R0. November 18, 2018 - June 26, 2019 (220d).
- R1. June 26, 2019 - June 10, 2021 (715d).
- RX. Based (no subnotes).
- M. #100266.
- I. 17 members (10 + 4 + 3).

More information: BBJProjeK The Agency (20251106).

BBJProjeK The Authority
Subsidiary entity of the BBJProjeK organization focused on desktop privacy and security development. Founded om May 2023, but the entity was legally set on date September 13th, 2023.

Core team of the BBJProjeK organization, alongside former members of ZoncEU, founded to host new internal projects focused on security and privacy development. It was also initially a placeholder for external documentation.

Created projects such as Swift Lyx Security, KanXras, the BBJProjeK Radio infrastructure, alongside many other projects and core development of the whole organization.

- R1. May 11, 2023 - TTT+ (910d+).

- I. 6 members (3 + 1 + 2).
