Hey all,
We've recently upgraded the whole radio infrastructure again, this time reallocting older resources and making the radio even more powerful, stable and dynamic.

We also updated the backend engine for our dynamic radios (The Labs X FM, Radio Authority Park, and The Redpill FM). This means that the radio plays content in a dynamic way, instead of being a prebuilt file.

Changelog for dynamic engine:
* src: Set custom parameters for individual songs
* src: Define quiet seconds
* data: Import new adverts and sound effects
* src: Allow dynamic outros with adverts
* src: Re-make intro and normalize volume accross commentaries

The new upgrade also includes a faster access to data and more coverage over with FM on supported locations.

Plans for 2026 are on the way and they're clear; keep releasing DLCs and updating content for our already existent radio stations.

Enjoy the new upcoming music!
