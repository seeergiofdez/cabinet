Hey everyone,

Just a quick note about the external image hosting we used to use for a while. You might have noticed that cubeupload.com has been down for a while.

To avoid any issues, please use one of these alternatives for new images:
* Our own CDN infrastructure (the best option!)
* Imgur (imgur.com)
* FileGarden (filegarden.com) - This is our new preferred external site.

What about old CubeUpload images? Don't worry, as we've proactively backed up and transferred all old images from CubeUpload over to FileGarden, so they are safe and accessible.

Need access to FileGarden?
We have a shared account to make things easy. If you need to upload something there, just reach out to the team on Slack, at #infra, and we'll get you the login details.

Thanks, all
Have a nice week
