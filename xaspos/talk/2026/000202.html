Today a new RaBBAu Internal Note released (2026-12).

The note specified the deprecation of Cypher Rose, and most importantly, other not mentioned hidden product, FlaTOR latest builds.

The continued existence of FlaTOR builds constitutes a clear policy violation, as FlaTOR was officially scheduled for deprecation in mid-2025.

Historically, FAT (FlaTOR Assessment and Termination) procedures were misapplied, serving as a pretext for unrelated activities and contributing directly to FlaTOR's operational failures. This rationale for its deprecation is documented in ticket 000501.

We partnered with the Ragyxo Foundation to ensure the complete removal of all FlaTOR code, eliminating both online availability and offline copies.

We're reassigning remaining resources used for for GitLab CE instance. Note effective on February 7, 2026.
