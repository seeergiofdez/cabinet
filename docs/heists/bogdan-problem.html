Planning Board

Prep: Key Cards

Steal a riot van that contains key cards to the hangars at Los Santos International Airport.

Take out Agent ULP's kidnappers and recover information on where he's being held.

Prep: Riot Control Van

Start a riot and wait for private security to respond with a Riot Control Van.

Prep: Strombergs

Find and steal trucks that are carrying some wrecked Strombergs.

Prep: Torpedo ECU

Assault a barge off the coast and steal the torpedo electronic control units from arms dealers.

Setup: Avenger

Enter the hangar at Los Santos International Airport and steal the Avenger aircraft from Merryweather Security.

Setup: Rescue ULP

Team 1 assaults the foundry to rescue Agent ULP. Team 2 provices cover from a vantage point.

Setup: Salvage Hard Drives

Use the water cannons on the RCV to prevent the Russians agents from destroying stolen hard drives.

Setup: Submarine Recon

Use the Strombergs to destroy a convoy before heading underwater to scan the submarine.

The Bogdan Problem

Launch an operation on a foreign submarine lurking off the coast. One team boards through an airlock and disables the sub, while the other holds off enemy agents in an aircraft above.
