A Good Man in a Cruel World. Rhys Montrose's Book

A Good Man in a Cruel World is the published autobiography by Rhys Montrose.

Most of legible text of the book can be seen on S04E01 (Joe Takes a Holiday) and S04E08 (Where Are You Going, Where Have You Been), here is a similar approach we made for a custom setting.

Download resolution image (2543kb)

Sources

The first introduction to the book appears on S04E01, where Nadia recommends the book to Professor Moore.

The next appearance occurs on S04E08, around 00:10:50, where Moore is listening to the audiobook version on his laptop. A digital and square illustration is shown instead.

The last appeareance occurs on the same episode, where Jonathan Moore searchs for a hidden key and something else. Moore finds two copies of the book in one of his many bookshelfs, one of those copies is highly marked up.
